Why the conversion isn't one to one

The two systems count different things. That is the whole reason a conversion factor exists, and it is why every honest converter returns an approximation rather than an exact figure.

Questions students actually ask

How many US credits is 1 ECTS?
Half a semester credit. The ratio falls out of the full-time load on each side: 60 ECTS is one European academic year, 30 semester credits is one US academic year.
Why isn't the conversion one to one?
The two systems count different things. One ECTS credit represents 25 to 30 hours of total student workload, independent study included. One US semester credit is built from classroom contact time 鈥?one hour of instruction plus at least two hours of outside work per week across roughly fifteen weeks, or about 45 hours in total. Because the units measure different activity, any conversion is an approximation rather than an exact equivalence.
How do I convert ECTS to US quarter credits?
Convert to semester credits first, then multiply by 1.5. One semester credit equals 1.5 quarter credits, so 60 ECTS is 30 semester credits, which is 45 quarter credits.
Will a 180 ECTS bachelor's be accepted for a US master's?
Often yes, but not automatically. The degree converts to about 90 semester credits against a US bachelor's requirement of 120鈥?30. Some graduate programmes accept Bologna-compliant three-year degrees as equivalent; others require a credential evaluation, and a few ask for additional coursework. The admissions page of the specific programme is the only reliable answer.
What if my university uses a different ratio?
Use it. Published institutional ratios override the general 2:1 baseline. Ratios of 1.5:1 and 3:1 both appear in practice, usually where contact hours are unusually high or low.
Can I submit a converted credit total to a university?
No. Calculator results are estimates for planning. Formal credit recognition is decided by the receiving institution, usually through a Learning Agreement for exchange study or a credential evaluation for degree admission. Use a converted figure to plan and to ask better questions, then confirm with the institution.
Is ECTS the same as an ECTS grade?
No. ECTS credits measure workload; ECTS grades measure performance. They are separate values on a transcript and convert by completely different rules.
Which countries use ECTS?
All members of the European Higher Education Area 鈥?the EU states plus Iceland, Norway, Turkey and others. A number of universities outside Europe have adopted it as well.
