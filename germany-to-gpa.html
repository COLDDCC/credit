Which number do you actually use?

The one your target programme requires. If the US programme accepts the transcript as-is, report the German grade and let them read it. Questions students actually ask

How do I convert a German grade to a US GPA?
Two standard routes exist. The modified Bavarian formula, used by German universities and uni-assist to convert INTO the German scale, can be run in reverse 鈥?a German 2.0 is roughly a 3.0. More commonly in the US, evaluators map bands: 1.0鈥?.5 to 4.0, 1.6鈥?.5 to 3.3鈥?.7, 2.6鈥?.5 to 2.3鈥?.0, 3.6鈥?.0 to 1.7鈥?.0. Both are estimates; WES evaluates course by course.
Is a German 2.0 a good grade?
Yes. The scale is inverted: 1.0 is the best and 4.0 is the minimum pass. A 2.0 is 'gut' (good), roughly a 3.3鈥?.7 US GPA. A 2.5 sits on the edge of good and is still respectable.
What is the modified Bavarian formula?

No. WES evaluates course by course and publishes no official table. The band values here are reconstructions of common practice for planning 鈥?your actual WES result is institution-specific.
Why is my German grade not a simple flip?
Because the scales don't line up one-to-one and the grading cultures differ. A German 1.0 means 'the best' rather than a specific percentage, and German grading is stricter at the top. Band approaches and the Bavarian formula give more defensible estimates than 5 鈭?grade.
