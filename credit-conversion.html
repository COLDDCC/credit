Why the conversion isn't one to one

The two systems count different things. That is the whole reason a conversion factor exists, and it is why every honest converter returns an approximation rather than an exact figure.

Divide the two full-time years into each other and you get the familiar 2:1 ratio: 60 ECTS a year against 30 semester credits a year. Compare raw workload hours instead and the ratio lands somewhere between 1.5:1 and 1.8:1.
