Why a Dutch 7 is a good grade

The Dutch scale runs 1 to 10 with 10 as 'excellent', but it is used differently from scales with the same shape. A 10 is reserved for near-perfection, 9s are exceptional, and the grades that students actually earn cluster between 6 and 8. A Dutch 8 is a genuinely strong result 鈥?an '8.0+ average' is the threshold many universities use for cum laude.
