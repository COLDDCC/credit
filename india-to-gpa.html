Why a 10-point CGPA doesn't map to a simple 4.0

The Indian 10-point scale looks proportional to the US 4.0 鈥?divide by 10, times 4 鈥?and the arithmetic is tempting. But the two scales behave differently at the top. Indian universities set their own banding, some award the top bands generously, and a 9.0 at a strict institution can mean more than a 9.5 at a generous one. Evaluators read the transcript, the institution, and the banding tables together.
